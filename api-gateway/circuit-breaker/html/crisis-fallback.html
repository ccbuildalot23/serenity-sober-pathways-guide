🧘‍♀️ Immediate Coping Techniques

While waiting for help, try these grounding techniques:

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste

Box Breathing

Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Repeat.

Safe Person

Call a trusted friend, family member, or support person

Safe Space

Go to a safe, comfortable location where you feel secure
