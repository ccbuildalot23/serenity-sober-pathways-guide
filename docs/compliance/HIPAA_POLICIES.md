## 1. Privacy Policy {#privacy-policy}

### Purpose
To ensure the privacy and security of Protected Health Information (PHI) in compliance with HIPAA Privacy Rule.

### Policy
- All PHI shall be used and disclosed only as permitted by HIPAA
- Minimum necessary principle applies to all PHI access
- Patients have the right to access, amend, and receive accounting of disclosures
- Privacy practices notice must be provided to all users

### Procedures
1. Obtain user consent before any PHI disclosure
2. Limit PHI access to minimum necessary for job functions
3. Respond to patient requests within 30 days
4. Document all disclosures in audit system

## 2. Security Policy {#security-policy}

### Purpose
To protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all ePHI.

### Policy
- All ePHI must be encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Access controls must be implemented and regularly reviewed
- Security incidents must be reported immediately
- Regular security risk assessments required

### Procedures
1. Enable encryption on all systems storing ePHI
2. Implement multi-factor authentication for administrative access
3. Conduct quarterly security reviews
4. Document and investigate all security incidents

---

## 3. Access Control Policy {#access-control-policy}

### Purpose
To ensure ePHI access is limited to authorized individuals.

### Policy
- Unique user identification required for all users
- Role-based access control (RBAC) must be implemented
- Automatic logoff after 30 minutes of inactivity
- Access reviews conducted quarterly

### Procedures
1. Create unique user accounts for each individual
2. Assign minimum necessary permissions based on role
3. Remove access immediately upon termination
4. Review access logs monthly

### User Roles
- **Patient**: Access to own records only
- **Support Member**: View access to assigned patients
- **Provider**: Full access to assigned patients
- **Administrator**: System configuration and user management

## 4. Workforce Training Policy {#workforce-training-policy}

### Purpose
To ensure all workforce members understand HIPAA requirements.

### Policy
- All workforce members must complete HIPAA training before accessing PHI
- Annual refresher training required
- Training records maintained for 6 years
- Sanctions for non-compliance

### Training Topics
1. HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules
2. Recognizing and reporting security incidents
3. Proper PHI handling procedures
4. Password security and access controls
5. Mobile device security
6. Phishing and social engineering awareness

### Procedures
1. New hire training within 30 days
2. Document training completion
3. Annual assessment of training effectiveness
4. Remedial training for violations

## 5. Incident Response Policy {#incident-response-policy}

### Purpose
To ensure proper response to security incidents and breaches.

### Policy
- All incidents reported within 24 hours
- Breach notification within 60 days if required
- Incident documentation retained for 6 years
- Root cause analysis for all incidents

### Incident Response Steps
1. **Immediate Response** (0-24 hours)
   - Contain the incident
   - Assess scope and impact
   - Notify Security Officer
   - Preserve evidence

2. **Investigation** (24-72 hours)
   - Determine root cause
   - Identify affected individuals
   - Assess notification requirements
   - Document findings

3. **Remediation** (72 hours - 30 days)
   - Implement fixes
   - Notify affected individuals if required
   - Report to HHS if required
   - Update policies/procedures

4. **Follow-up** (30-60 days)
   - Verify remediation effectiveness
   - Conduct lessons learned
   - Update training materials
   - Monitor for recurrence

## 6. Data Backup and Recovery Policy {#data-backup-policy}

### Purpose
To ensure ePHI availability and disaster recovery capability.

### Policy
- Daily automated backups required
- Backup encryption mandatory
- Monthly restoration testing
- Offsite backup storage required

### Procedures
1. Configure automated daily backups
2. Verify backup completion daily
3. Test restoration monthly
4. Document restoration procedures
5. Maintain 3-2-1 backup strategy:
   - 3 copies of data
   - 2 different storage media
   - 1 offsite location

### Recovery Objectives
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 24 hours

## 7. Device and Media Control Policy {#device-media-policy}

### Purpose
To govern the use and disposal of devices containing ePHI.

### Policy
- All devices must be encrypted
- Inventory of all devices maintained
- Secure disposal procedures required
- BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) prohibited for PHI access

### Procedures
1. Encrypt all laptops and mobile devices
2. Track device assignments
3. Wipe devices before disposal
4. Obtain certificate of destruction
5. Prohibit USB storage devices

## 8. PHI Disclosure Policy {#phi-disclosure-policy}

### Purpose
To ensure appropriate disclosure of PHI.

### Policy
- Written authorization required for most disclosures
- Minimum necessary standard applies
- Accounting of disclosures maintained
- Verification of requestor identity required

### Permitted Disclosures Without Authorization
1. To the individual
2. For treatment, payment, or healthcare operations
3. Required by law
4. Public health activities
5. Health oversight activities
6. Judicial proceedings
7. Law enforcement (limited)
8. Serious threat to health/safety

### Procedures
1. Verify identity of requestor
2. Confirm authorization validity
3. Apply minimum necessary
4. Document disclosure in system
5. Provide accounting when requested

## 9. Audit and Monitoring Policy {#audit-policy}

### Purpose
To monitor compliance with HIPAA requirements.

### Policy
- All ePHI access logged
- Regular audit log reviews
- Suspicious activity investigation
- Annual compliance assessments

### Audit Requirements
1. **System Access Logs**
   - User authentication
   - PHI access attempts
   - Configuration changes
   - Security events

2. **Review Schedule**
   - Daily: Security alerts
   - Weekly: Failed access attempts
   - Monthly: User activity
   - Quarterly: Compliance assessment

3. **Documentation**
   - Finding reports
   - Remediation plans
   - Trend analysis
   - Executive summaries

## 10. Business Associate Management Policy {#ba-policy}

### Purpose
To ensure business associates protect PHI appropriately.

### Policy
- BAA required before PHI disclosure
- Annual BA security reviews
- Immediate breach notification required
- Right to terminate for non-compliance

### Procedures
1. Identify all business associates
2. Execute BAA before PHI sharing
3. Review BA security practices
4. Monitor BA compliance
5. Document all BA relationships
6. Annual BAA review and update
