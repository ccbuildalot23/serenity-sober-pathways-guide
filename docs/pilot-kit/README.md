#### 15‑Minute Demo Script

1) Login and navigate to Provider Dashboard
2) Open ROI & Billing Hints panel
   - Explain retained patients estimate and time captured (CCM/BHI)
   - Show suggested codes with confidence
3) Click “Download claim checklist (CSV)”
   - Explain monthly CSV and evidence requirements
4) Review security posture (headers, role checks)
5) Confirm success criteria and schedule a quick follow‑up

#### Pilot Success Criteria

- At least 1 provider downloads CSV successfully
- Provider validates suggested codes align with activity
- Provider can explain ROI estimate and time captured
- No P0/P1 defects reported during the pilot window
- Post‑merge smoke passes nightly for CSV endpoint
