# Pilot Playbook

## Inclusion criteria
- Adults with SUD, high ED utilization, frequent no-shows, or gaps in care.
- Providers offering CCM/BHI-capable care navigation.

## Readmission-risk metrics
- 30-day readmission rate, ED visits per 30 days, missed appointments.

## Escalation tree
- Frontline navigator → Provider → Practice admin → Medical director.

## Feedback cadence
- Daily: navigator huddles; Weekly: provider review; Monthly: leadership KPI review.

## Enabling care-navigation billing
- Train staff on CCM (99490/99439) and BHI (99484) documentation.
- Track minutes via interaction events.

## KPIs
- Provider satisfaction, patient engagement, minutes captured, 30-day readmission rate.

Anchors: provider practice-expansion & care-navigation billing.
